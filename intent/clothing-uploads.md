# FitLab: Upload existing clothing and outfits

Status: Draft — pending team review.
Named team approver: Pending.

## Purpose

Let users bring clothing or outfits they already own into the styling experience.

## User experience

Users can upload images of their clothing or outfits in addition to requesting invented looks. Working scope: clothing-only photos, such as flat lays or items on hangers, rather than photos of people. Generated results continue to use fictional mannequins.

Both workflows are supported: upload individual pieces to build outfits around, or upload a complete outfit to improve. Uploads are saved in the user's account-based wardrobe for reuse across sessions and devices.

Selected uploaded pieces must be preserved as closely as possible in generated looks, including their visible colors, patterns, shape, and distinguishing details. They may be changed or replaced only when the user requests it. This is a fidelity requirement, not a guarantee of exact reproduction.

## Success criteria

- Users can upload and preview clothing-only images.
- Unsupported or failed uploads receive a clear explanation.
- The interface explains the clothing-only requirement before upload.
- Users can select individual uploaded pieces as the starting point for outfit suggestions.
- Users can submit a complete uploaded outfit with requests to improve or change it.
- Users can reopen and reuse their uploaded pieces and outfits from their account on another device.
- Generation uses selected uploads as visual references, and critique compares the generated image with those references to check preservation.
- If selected clothing conflicts with the occasion or weather, the app explains the conflict rather than silently replacing it.

## Open decisions

- Supported file formats, size limits, and handling of images containing people.
