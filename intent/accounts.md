# FitLab: Personal accounts

Status: Draft — pending team review.
Named team approver: Pending.

## Purpose

Give users persistent access to their saved looks and uploaded wardrobe across sessions and devices.

## User experience

Users can create an account, sign in, and sign out. After signing in, they can access their own saved looks, individual clothing uploads, and complete outfit uploads.

An account and active sign-in are required before using outfit generation, uploads, remixing, or saved looks. There is no guest generation workflow in the first version.

## Success criteria

- The same account can retrieve its saved looks and wardrobe on another device.
- Saved looks and uploads are associated with their owner's account.
- Users cannot access another user's private saved looks or uploads.
- Sign-in and save failures are clearly explained.
- Unauthenticated users must sign in before accessing styling features or private wardrobe data; this requirement is enforced beyond the interface.
